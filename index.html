Getting started

Up and running in minutes

1

Download & sign in with iCloud

Install LinkReader on your iPhone or Mac. No account needed — it uses your existing Apple ID and iCloud.

2

Add your OpenAI API key (iPhone) or install OpenVox (Mac)

Paste your OpenAI key in Settings for iPhone audio generation. On Mac, install OpenVox to generate audio locally at no per-article cost.

3

Share an article link to LinkReader

In Safari, tap Share → LinkReader. The article is fetched, processed, and ready to listen to — usually in under a minute.

4

Listen anywhere

Play on your iPhone, Mac, AirPods, or through CarPlay. Pick up exactly where you left off across devices.
