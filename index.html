Safari Extension

One tap from any page

The LinkReader Safari extension is included free with the app. Enable it once and send articles to your queue from anywhere on the web.

1

iPhone — enable in Settings

Go to Settings → Safari → Extensions, find LinkReader, and turn it on. Grant access to all websites when prompted.

2

Mac — enable in Safari Preferences

Open Safari → Settings → Extensions, tick LinkReader, and allow it to read pages you visit.

3

Send any article instantly

On iPhone tap the Share button → LinkReader. On Mac click the LinkReader toolbar icon. The article is queued and audio generation begins.

Getting started

Up and running in minutes

1

Download & sign in with iCloud

Install LinkReader on your iPhone or Mac. No account needed — it uses your existing Apple ID and iCloud.

2

Add your OpenAI API key (iPhone) or install OpenVox (Mac)

Paste your OpenAI key in Settings for iPhone audio generation. On Mac, install OpenVox to generate audio locally at no per-article cost.

3

Share an article link to LinkReader

In Safari, tap Share → LinkReader. The article is fetched, processed, and ready to listen to — usually in under a minute.

4

Listen anywhere

Play on your iPhone, Mac, AirPods, or through CarPlay. Pick up exactly where you left off across devices.
